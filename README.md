
# Freshco Hypermarket – E-commerce Delivery Operations & Customer Insights Analysis

## Project Overview

Freshco Hypermarket launched a home delivery service to improve customer convenience and expand its operational reach. With increasing order volumes, the company needed deeper visibility into delivery operations, customer behavior, completion rates, and product trends.

This project focuses on analyzing order-level and customer-level data to uncover actionable insights that can improve delivery efficiency, optimize operations, and enhance customer satisfaction.

---

# Business Problem

Although Freshco had large amounts of transactional and operational data, the business lacked structured reporting and analysis to answer critical questions such as:

* Which time slots and delivery areas experience the highest demand?
* What factors impact order completion rates?
* Which customer acquisition sources generate high-value customers?
* How does delivery performance affect customer satisfaction?
* Which operational bottlenecks lead to delays and inefficiencies?

Without these insights, it became difficult for the business to optimize delivery operations and improve customer experience.

---

# Objective

The primary objective of this project was to:

* Analyze delivery operations and customer behavior
* Measure completion rates and delivery efficiency
* Evaluate customer lifetime value (LTV)
* Identify demand patterns across time slots and locations
* Explore product-level trends and order composition
* Generate actionable recommendations for operational improvement

# Analytical Approach

## 1. Order-Level Analysis

Analyzed:

* Demand distribution across time slots
* Monthly order trends
* Delivery area performance
* Order volume patterns

## 2. Completion Rate Analysis

Evaluated:

* Successful vs failed deliveries
* Time slots with lower completion rates
* Operational inefficiencies affecting fulfillment

## 3. Customer-Level Analysis

Measured:

* Customer Lifetime Value (LTV)
* Repeat customer behavior
* Acquisition source performance
* Customer retention patterns

## 4. Delivery Performance Analysis

Examined:

* Delivery delays
* Pickup bottlenecks
* Delivery timelines
* Area-wise delivery efficiency

## 5. Product-Level Analysis

Explored:

* Order composition trends
* Product count impact on delivery time
* Relationship between order size and customer ratings

# Key Insights

## Operational Insights

* Identified high-demand delivery slots and delivery areas
* Found specific time periods with lower completion rates
* Detected operational bottlenecks causing delivery delays
* Highlighted inefficient delivery zones requiring optimization

## Customer Insights

* Measured customer lifetime value across acquisition channels
* Identified high-value customer acquisition sources
* Observed relationship between delivery experience and customer ratings

## Delivery Insights

* Longer delivery timelines negatively impacted customer satisfaction
* Larger orders often increased delivery delays
* Certain time slots consistently showed better operational efficiency
